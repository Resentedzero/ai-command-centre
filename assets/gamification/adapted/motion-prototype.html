Notices

Working loops at 100 ms per frame; idle at 2 fps. The seal pulses amber every 1.6 s and the runes step every 2 s. An event brightens its room one level for 400 ms. Failure and stop flash once, then hold red; completion flashes green once. With reduced motion, sprites hold their first frame and state stays readable in the light, marker and label.
