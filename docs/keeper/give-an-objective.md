# Give an agent an objective

Keywords: objective, autonomous, delegate, let it work, idea architect, loop, iterations, finish

On an agent's page, choose **Give an objective**. Write what should be accomplished, choose which thinking actions it may take and which of its keys it may use (and how often), and start.

The agent then works on its own. Each iteration it:

1. reads a compact ledger of what it has done so far,
2. decides one next action: think (plan, brainstorm, analyse, compare, critique, write), use a granted capability, ask you for approval, or finish,
3. carries it out under governance,
4. records the result.

It stops when it decides the objective is met, or at its limits: 12 iterations, 15 active minutes (approval waits do not count), or when the budget cannot cover another iteration and the final write. It then writes a deliverable that says why it stopped.

Watch progress in the workflow run's **Autonomous work** panel. An emergency stop halts it at the next action. Autonomous runs are never retried automatically.
